# Safety — Lock-out / Tag-out Procedure for Compressors

## Before any maintenance
Stop the compressor with the controller stop button and wait until the motor has fully stopped.
Switch off and lock the main isolating switch, and attach a personal tag with your name and the date.
Close the air outlet valve and depressurise the compressor completely; verify zero pressure on the gauge.
Wait at least 10 minutes after stopping before opening the canopy, because oil and internal parts can be hot.

## Restarting after maintenance
Remove tools, refit all guards, then remove your own lock and tag. Only the person who placed a lock may remove it.
