# AX-200 Air Compressor — Service Manual

## Maintenance intervals
The oil must be changed every 4000 running hours or once a year, whichever comes first.
The air filter element must be replaced every 2000 running hours, or earlier in dusty environments.
The oil separator element must be replaced every 8000 running hours.
After any maintenance, reset the service interval counter in the controller menu Service > Reset.

## Approved consumables
Use only the synthetic compressor oil AX-Synth 46. Mixing oil types can damage the oil separator.
The oil capacity of the AX-200 is 12 litres.

## Installation requirements
Leave at least 1 metre of free space on all sides of the compressor for cooling air and service access.
The ambient temperature in the compressor room must stay between 0 and 46 degrees Celsius.
The compressor must stand on a level floor that can carry 900 kilograms.

## Pressure settings
The default working pressure of the AX-200 is 7.5 bar.
The maximum allowed working pressure is 10 bar; never set the unloading pressure above this value.
To change the unloading pressure, open the controller menu Settings > Pressure and enter the new value.
