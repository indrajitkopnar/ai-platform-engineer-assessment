# AX Controller — Alarm and Error Codes

## E104 High discharge temperature
Alarm E104 means the discharge temperature exceeded 110 degrees Celsius and the compressor stopped to protect itself.
Check that the cooler is clean, the cooling fan runs and the room ventilation is sufficient.
Check the oil level; low oil also causes high discharge temperature.

## E212 Pressure sensor fault
Alarm E212 means the outlet pressure sensor signal is missing or out of range.
Check the sensor cable and connector first; replace the pressure sensor if the cable is intact.

## E301 Motor overload
Alarm E301 means the main motor current exceeded the overload relay setting.
Check the supply voltage on all three phases and verify that the motor turns freely.

## W020 Service due
Warning W020 means the service interval counter has expired.
Perform the scheduled maintenance and reset the counter; the warning does not stop the compressor.
